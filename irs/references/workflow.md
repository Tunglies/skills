# Implementation And Review Workflow

## Handoffs

- **Writer:** Gets shape, scope, non-goals, behavior boundary, budget, and validation. It alone edits and never commits.
- **Correctness reviewer:** Gets acceptance conditions, behavior boundary, scope, full diff, and validation. Withhold writer rationale unless factually necessary.
- **Simplification reviewer:** Gets behavior boundary, non-goals, budget, full diff, and validation evidence, without the other reviewer's conclusions.
- **Main agent:** Records state and findings, reads the diff, dispatches gates, validates, and performs authorized delivery. It is not a second writer.

Use distinct fresh reviewers. If delegation is unavailable, the main agent may write but must identify missing independent gates.

Delegate bounded independent reads, caller analysis, or validation when they can improve coverage or reduce elapsed work. Give each delegate its scope, snapshot, output, and stop condition; the main agent integrates results and resolves disagreements. Keep edits with the sole writer, and avoid concurrent checks that mutate shared build or generated state. Do not spawn agents for trivial work that the current role can finish directly, or wait idle while independent work remains.

If the writer is unavailable on resume, first establish that it is no longer writing, inspect its partial diff, and record an explicit transfer to one replacement writer (which may be the main agent). Preserve the contract and findings and revalidate affected gates. Ask only if ownership or partial changes cannot be resolved, not merely because a prior session's agent no longer exists.

## Execution

1. Read repository instructions and relevant code. Record baseline, worktree fingerprint, and pre-existing dirty paths; search callers and indirect contracts. On a durable resume, use `dev-handoff` to revalidate drift before choosing the first live gate.
2. When applicable, use `cross-repo-integrator` to bind every repository and dependency edge to this contract, and use `repo-validate` to establish the nontrivial validation plan. These companions enrich the contract; they do not create writers or parallel review loops.
3. For Implement, the IRS-designated sole writer returns changed files, budget variance, and exact validation; Review existing skips the writer. Preserve writer identity across phases and corrections, using the transfer procedure above only when needed.
4. Inspect the working candidate against goal, non-goals, behavior, budget, edge/failure paths, new structures or ownership, validation, and unrelated artifacts. Return blockers to the IRS-designated sole writer. Any review here is preliminary if a later commit will repartition content into the index.
5. When commit or amend is authorized, have `git-delivery` perform its read-only index-isolation preflight and construct the exact staged-delivery candidate before final gates. Record the original index, authorized patch/hunks, staged-delivery candidate tree identity, and worktree fingerprint. For amend, also require explicit rewrite authorization and record the exact old commit (normally current HEAD; stop if another target is unsupported), its parent, and the complete replacement tree. If exact isolation is unavailable, stop before changing the index.
6. Final correctness review covers the full current candidate, requested behavior, drift, edge/error paths, cross-module contracts, tests, and validation gaps.
7. Simplification review checks unnecessary files, state, APIs, abstractions, helpers, duplication, budget overruns, and complexity required for safety. Run it alongside correctness on the same stable candidate when independent; defer it when unresolved correctness issues would invalidate its premise. Integrate both results before accepting edits or declaring readiness.
8. After all accepted edits receive fresh full correctness, validate that candidate and establish its readiness lock. Missing required evidence remains explicit and prevents unsupported readiness claims.
9. `git-delivery` may consume only the locked staged-delivery candidate for an authorized commit or amend. Perform other separately authorized delivery actions, then final handoff or Close when requested.

Record findings as ID, `blocking` or `optional`, evidence, `accepted`/`deferred`/`rejected`, reason, and owner. Only accepted blockers or behavior-preserving simplifications trigger edits.

After a blocker, the IRS-designated sole writer fixes it, any staged-delivery candidate is reconstructed, affected validation reruns, and the full candidate receives final correctness review. After every accepted simplification, that sole writer applies it. Any candidate-byte change, whether code, docs, config, generated output, metadata, or another file type, requires candidate reconstruction when applicable and fresh full correctness on the updated candidate. Rereview may be skipped only when candidate content is byte-for-byte unchanged.

If scope exceeds an estimated budget but remains inside the authorized outcome, record the reason and continue. A user-imposed hard cap remains binding. Ask before exceeding that cap or introducing behavior, public contract changes, ownership changes, or external effects outside the request; continue work that does not depend on that decision.

Use repository-native validation and make diff hygiene candidate-phase aware. For a staged-delivery candidate, check the exact staged patch against its recorded baseline with `git diff --cached --check` or an equivalent locked candidate-tree/patch check; plain `git diff --check` inspects unstaged changes and cannot validate or substitute for the staged-delivery candidate. For a working candidate, check each applicable candidate component: staged, unstaged, and relevant untracked content. Record any component that cannot be checked rather than implying one command covered it. For nontrivial evidence, `repo-validate` owns the revision-bound ledger and updates the contract with failures and stale conditions. When commit or amend is authorized, final validation must bind to and actually exercise the staged-delivery candidate; unrelated unstaged work cannot substitute for it, and worktree changes outside it make evidence non-final when they can affect the check. Static checks do not prove runtime, device, production, or performance behavior. A validation finding does not authorize the validator to edit; an accepted in-scope blocker returns to the IRS-designated sole writer.

Commit, amend, push, tag, release, and PR creation require separate authorization. For an authorized commit or amend, `git-delivery` prepares the staged-delivery candidate before final gates and later consumes its readiness lock without reconstructing or rerunning unchanged IRS gates. Commit or amend only when the current index tree and patch equal the locked staged-delivery candidate; amend also requires the recorded old commit, parent, complete replacement tree, and explicit rewrite authorization to remain unchanged. Any index-tree, staging, hook, file, or candidate change after the readiness lock returns to the earliest affected gate. Report local and remote state separately. Tags and releases remain outside Git delivery.

For a cross-repository consumer that requires a newly reachable source identity, the same Canonical Contract may define an intermediate source checkpoint. First, `git-delivery` prepares the exact staged-delivery source candidate under its index-isolation rules. Source-scoped final correctness, simplification, and validation establish its readiness lock. Then `git-delivery` consumes only that locked staged-delivery candidate for the separately authorized exact source commit, push, or PR actions needed for reachability. Bind passed source gates to the delivered identity, refresh the contract and worktree fingerprints, and resume the designated writer at the consumer gate without closing the workflow or repeating unchanged source gates. Candidate changes reopen affected gates. Missing delivery authorization or reachability blocks the dependent transition; continue independent authorized local work without claiming final consumer resolution.

Resume at the first gate affected by changed candidate content, acceptance or preservation requirements, evidence, or authorization. Unchanged bytes do not establish coverage of a new requirement: inspect existing review and validation coverage, and reopen only affected gates. Do not repeat unaffected gates.

Once required reviews and proportionate checks pass, finish the authorized outcome. Broaden or repeat validation only for new changes, failures, or unresolved concerns. Optional suggestions alone do not reopen the correction loop.

When durable capture is requested or needed, a pause or stop may interrupt any gate. After the current atomic action, capture the partial gate, worktree fingerprint, findings, evidence, authorization, and next stop condition immediately; do not wait for technical closure or delivery. Ordinary same-turn continuation does not create a handoff. Final handoff and Close occur after their applicable technical and delivery gates.
